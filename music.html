MAT111AU Works

MAT111AU - Audio Engineering & Design was a course offered as part of the Media Arts & Design minor at UCSB, and during it I learned the basics of Ableton, a software for Electronic Music composition. My pieces for the class weren't too great, but hey, it's a learning process.

This is a remix of the track Cold Strive by Skelpolu. The name is inspired by Sonic the Hedgehog, as the original song reminded me of that series' music for some reason, and so I took as much creative liberty as I could to make the remix unique like how Sonic games make their Act 1's and Act 2's distinct. In my opinion, there's just not that much point to making a remix of someone else's work if you aren't going to make it your own.
This is a cover of a song by one of my favorite Electronic artists under the alias of Wrillez Basics. I tried to keep the instrumentation simple and snappy to match the feel of the original, and I'd say it makes for a pretty decent loop. The style of Wrillez Basics overall is greatly influential to myself and I see it coming through on many of my future tracks.
A spooky loop I made with just two Ableton tracks and a bit of panning. Probably video game material, thinking like Luigi's Mansion, but not for any of my projects.
Hope this doesn't wreck your speakers. This, along with the next track, Impossibility, are more or less vent art made apparent by their intensity. Exception, AKA Burnout, was made entirely using Ableton's built-in S&H Games instrument, which I found to be very discordant and fitting for unsettling tracks like this one. After the initial explosion of energy, the track quickly fades with only the fast-paced, muted blips remaining, like how humans tend to have outbursts in response to extreme stress followed by stillness with those same uneasy thoughts remaining for extended periods of time.
The final project. As of creating this, I was feeling quite negative in regards to my Ableton experience. Lack of Music Theory, my unsatisfaction with the built-in instruments, the deadline approaching, etc. led me to whip up a more abstract piece representing my disappointment. The track begins with a simple intro composed of harsh strings and bass, suggesting a more natural structure to follow, until a very loud, ear-grating sound interrupts you.
After a brief silence, the track resumes with a series of sweeping strings, until the familiar S&H Games sound joins it and grows louder until suddenly cutting off. Another period of silence, and then a depressing-sounding synth beat emerges with some Percussive Presets over it.
This track could somewhat represent the traditional Stages of Grief: Denial, with the normal albeit sloppy-sounding start, Anger, with the high-energy jumpscare that follows, Bargaining, with the mysterious-sounding sweeping synths, Depression, with the third section, and at the very end... Acceptance? I did turn this track in for full credit, but I did have to accept the fact that it wasn't up to my usual standards.
And before you ask, yes, that is my real voice.
