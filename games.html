A Psychological Horror/Survival game inspired by Baldi's Basics in Education and Learning. Play as a student with severe anxiety and sensory sensitivity and navigate a surreal retro-styled Schoolhouse whilst being pursued by monstrous versions of your teacher and peers. This game has been development for over two years and is still currently in Alpha; while I can not say when it will be completed, it has been a wonderful learning experience thus far.

A web-based, top-down, dystopian RPG created for the Web Narratives course offered as part of the Media Arts & Design minor at UCSB. Take the role of one of many imprisoned at a facility goverened by sentient AI, forced to work in a virtual world a la The Matrix. But when an opportunity presents itself to escape your cell, what will you make of it...? Game expected to fully release in 2027.
